MINIFORGE // SIMPLIFIED

The same machine, told as a story.

Miniforge's architecture is dense — a workflow FSM, a DAG orchestrator, policy gates, evidence bundles. These three stories tell the whole thing in one shared world of robots, doorkeepers, stickers and passes, so a human can actually grok it. Each ends with a grown-up translation table mapping every story element to the real component.

READING ORDER

Three stories, one world.

Read in order — later stories reuse the earlier ones' characters. Together they are the simplicity invariant: if a change to the architecture cannot be told in the story, the change is suspect.

  • 01 The Fort-Building Robots The factory itself: the garage board (work kanban), the coach (orchestrator), the wiggle test (gates), the two notebooks (evidence + event stream), the porch (operator console).
  • 02 The Robot Helpers and the Sticker Rules Policy and routing: gullible robots write request slips, a doorkeeper with no ears decides, stickers ride every paper, and helpers get crossed off before the contest starts.
  • 03 Your House, Their Clubhouse, and the Passes Tenancy, ownership and rights: every thing lives in exactly one house, joining prints passes instead of moving houses, and no switch ever bolts your own door.
