OPERATING PRINCIPLES

A factory, not a chatbot.

Miniforge models software delivery as an industrial process. Work is tracked as first-class artifacts, decisions are recorded, and every change flows through explicit quality and safety gates.

  • 01 Local-first Runs on your machine or inside your network, directly on your repositories. You control where it runs, what it can change, and how far it goes.
  • 02 Governed pipeline No single-prompt magic. Specialized agent roles move work through plan, implement, verify, review and release — with policy enforcement at every transition.
  • 03 Evidence trail Every decision, gate result and release is recorded and traceable. The output is real repositories and real deployments, not demos.
  • 04 Meta loop The factory observes its own failures, reviews and corrections, and uses those signals to improve how it plans, validates and releases work.
