PRODUCT LINE // 2026

One forge, several machines.

Miniforge is the factory. Around it: coordination for running factories at scale, an operator console, and an evaluation workbench.

Unit 01 · Available · Apache 2.0

Miniforge

The autonomous software factory. Write a spec, get a pull request — planned, implemented, verified, reviewed and released through a governed pipeline with a full evidence trail. Local-first: your machine, your network, your repositories.
Unit 02 · In development

Miniforge Fleet

Coordination and governance for factories at scale — many concurrent runs, shared policy, on-prem Kubernetes deployment shapes for organizations that run miniforge as infrastructure.

Unit 03 · In development

Miniforge Control

The operator console — terminal and native macOS. Watch runs move down the line, inspect gate results and evidence, and intervene when the factory asks for a human decision.

Unit 04 · Built · OSS release pending

Minibench

The evaluation workbench. Cross-product state validation and benchmarking — measuring how well the factory actually performs, so changes to the process are driven by evidence, not vibes.

PROOF

Built with itself.

Miniforge ships changes to its own codebase through its own pipeline. The architecture portfolio documents how the machines are built.
